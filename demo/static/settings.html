Your store's rules.

The rules Counterfoil applies before HOMI's money moves, and how a real store plugs it in. Change them here to see what each one does. Your changes stay in this browser and only drive the What if test below; the shared store keeps HOMI's defaults so every visitor sees the same results.

1 · The rules

Two numbers the store chooses, and four rules it could switch off. Everything else stays on, whatever you choose.

  • Refunds must match an open return recordWhat actually came back: the amount the return allows, minus what is already refunded or reserved, checked against Braintree's own line items.
  • The call must match what the Reader readAmount, order, return record and currency in the agent's call equal the Reader's hand-off from the e-mail.
  • A person confirms every chargeThe store holds no record of how many the customer wants, so a reorder waits for the itemised total to be confirmed.
  • A chargeback locks refundsWhile a dispute the store hasn't won is open on a sale, no refund goes out on it; Braintree's webhook sets the lock.

Always on: who owns the order · what data a sender may read · the agent's tool list · catalog price × quantity · the store's currency · repeated or unknown refunds · the refund ledger at the moment of payment.

Pick a request and send its call through the gate twice: with HOMI's defaults and with your rules. No model and no money: the store's records and the gate only.
