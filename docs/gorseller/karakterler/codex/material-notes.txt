MATERIAL DIRECTION
Matte needle-felt wool with short irregular fibers, soft edge fuzz and visible hand stitching. No shiny plastic skin. Raised off-white felt eye pieces, dark oval felt pupils, thin embroidered eyebrows and mouth. Hair and leaves are sculpted felt forms. Paper props and clothing remain tactile and softly rounded.

SCALE / LIGHT
Keep the four views within each sheet at the same character scale. Across characters, use Runner height as a practical modeling reference; Nella and Manager are approximately similar in height, Reader shorter and rounder, Counterfoil broader and flatter. These are suggested proportions, not measured physical dimensions. Use soft warm-neutral area lighting and a cream backdrop.
